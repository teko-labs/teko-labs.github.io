What we make

Inky Quest Game

A bounce puzzle for phones. Inky the octopus bounces by herself; you steer her left and right, match her colour to break the tiles, then take the pearls. Forty-seven levels across seven zones, from a sunlit reef to the deep end. Coming to Android first, with iPhone to follow. Free, with no adverts and nothing to buy.
J JuntoCash In the making

Budgeting and bill splitting in one app. Split a dinner with friends and your share counts in your own food budget, so your spending picture is whole for once. It works without an account, with everything kept on your phone; share a group by link when you want to. For Android and iPhone, with no bank linking and no adverts.

About

Teko Labs is run by one developer. It makes Inky Quest, a puzzle game, and JuntoCash, a budgeting app.

The apps have no adverts and do not sell data. What each one sends off your phone is listed in its privacy policy, and can be switched off.
